# Image du sommaire de la visite guidée — prompt Flow (12 septembre 2026)

Où elle va : plein écran derrière le sommaire quand un chanteur touche
« Revoir la visite guidée ». Même esprit que les images du site : **un seul objet,
une douche de lumière dorée, la brume, le noir**, le titre en bas à gauche, le
logo petit en bas à droite. L'objet, ici : une main qui touche un bouton de
lumière suspendu dans l'air. La visite est interactive, l'image le dit.

1. `logo.png` (le B doré dans son cercle) — dans `5 - Site web/`
2. `site-images/06-karaoke.jpg` (l'atelier, le micro) — pour le Style Lock :
   même noir, même or, même brume, même grain, même place du titre et du logo

**Fichiers attendus dans `5 - Site web/site-images/` :**
- `visite-sommaire-paysage.jpg` (16:9)
- `visite-sommaire-portrait.jpg` (9:16)

## Prompt A — PAYSAGE, format 16:9

> Use the two attached reference images. The first one is the official "Bohème Production" logo, a golden calligraphic letter B inside a golden circle. **Reproduce this logo exactly as it is, identical, unchanged, same shape, same lettering, same golden gradient. Do not redraw it, do not stylise it, do not alter a single letter.** The second one is a poster from the same series: **match its look exactly, same deep black stage, same single warm golden spotlight from above, same thin theatrical haze, same fine film grain, same placement of the title bottom-left and the small logo bottom-right.**
>
> Subject: a single elegant human hand, seen from the side, entering the frame from the right, index finger extended, the fingertip just touching a small glowing golden ring of light that floats in mid-air at the centre of the frame, like a button of pure light suspended in the darkness. From the point of contact, two or three thin concentric golden ripples spread outward, as if the air itself were a touchscreen. Only one hand, no body, no face, no sleeve detail, skin lit warmly by the golden glow.
> Action: the exact instant of the touch; tiny golden dust particles rise around the ring.
> Environment: an empty black theatre stage floor, a single warm golden spotlight falling from above through thin haze, vast empty darkness all around, the middle-left and upper part of the image kept dark and calm.
> Style: cinematic still, high-end editorial photography, luxurious and restrained, palette of deep black and warm gold only.
> Light: one soft golden spotlight from above, the glowing ring as a second warm light source on the fingertip, gentle rim light on the hand, no harsh highlights.
> Lens: 85mm at f/2.8, shallow depth of field, the fingertip and the ring perfectly sharp, background softly falling into black, fine film grain.
>
> Bottom-left, engraved in elegant thin golden serif capitals, large, perfectly spelled in French: "LA VISITE GUIDÉE".
> Just below it, much smaller, in thin letter-spaced golden capitals, perfectly spelled in French: "suis le guide, touche, écoute".
> Place the reference logo small in the bottom-right corner, at about 9% of the image width, glowing softly, its transparent background blending into the black.
> No other text than specified, no watermark, no face, no second hand, no phone, no screen frame.
